# Introduction to watsonx.governance

watsonx governance helps you direct, manage, and monitor your AI (LLMs, ML models, and even agents) so they are responsible, transparent, and compliant with regulations and internal policies<br>


By the end of this lab you will be able to:<br>
- Create a **use case** in watsonx.governance for your Sales Assistant
- **Register models and endpoints** (LLM, tuned model, or ADK service) under this use case
- Upload or connect **evaluation datasets** (prompts, responses, and labels)
- Run **evaluation jobs** (quality, safety, and bias checks) and interpret the results
- Define simple **policies and thresholds** (e.g., max toxicity, min quality score)
- Understand where watsonx.governance fits in the overall **agentic solution lifecycle**
