Sales Enablement Agent

Role: Frontline “copilot” for sales reps
Goal: Turn data & recommendations into scripts, playbooks, answers in real time.

Inputs
- Query from sales rep via chatbot (“What should I offer to Pak Budi?”)
- Customer profile, segment & recommended products (from #2 & #3)
- Knowledge base: FAQs, objection handling, regulatory text

Actions
- Decompose request into subtasks: fetch profile → call Product Matching → pull KB snippets
- Craft personalized pitch, cross-sell ideas, talking points
- Provide next-best-action suggestions (call, email, follow-up reminder, etc.)

Outputs
- Chatbot responses, sales scripts, email/WhatsApp templates
- Guided conversation flows inside CRM / portal
- Objection Handling
